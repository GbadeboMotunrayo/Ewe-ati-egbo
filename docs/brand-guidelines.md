# Ewe ati Egbo — Brand Guidelines

Source: PRD §1, §4. Working brand; the sponsor may supply a final commercial name.

## Name

**Ewe ati Egbo** — Yoruba for *"leaves and roots."* Gives the platform cultural roots
without locking it to one tradition forever, and reads as exactly what it sells:
botanicals. English tagline: *"Leaves and roots, sold with trust."*

## Positioning line

*The UK marketplace for traditional botanicals, herbs and natural wellness products —
verified sellers, honest labels, no fake cures.*

## Colour palette

Earthy, botanical, premium — not clinical, not pharmacy-white.

| Swatch | Hex | Name | Use |
|--------|-----|------|-----|
| 🟢 | `#1F6B3B` | Deep Green | Primary brand, CTAs, verified states, growth |
| 🟡 | `#C6862B` | Earth Ochre | Secondary/accent, highlights, roots & barks |
| 🟠 | `#E0A458` | Warm Sand | Backgrounds, cards, warmth |
| 🟤 | `#3A2A1E` | Bark Brown | Primary text, dark UI |
| ⚪ | `#F7F3EC` | Cream | Backgrounds, whitespace |
| 🔴 | `#C1442E` | Clay Red | Warnings, blocked claims, safety alerts (used sparingly) |

Traffic-light meanings (🟢 green / 🟠 amber / 🔴 red) map to the compliance engine —
the brand reinforces its own trust system.

## Typography

- **Headings:** a warm, confident geometric sans (e.g. Poppins / Sora) — legible on
  entry-level Android screens, consistent with the house pattern.
- **Body:** same family regular, optimised for readability of structured product info.

## Brand voice

- **Trustworthy and plain.** We say what a product is, where it's from, what's in it.
  We never imply it cures anything.
- **Culturally fluent, not gatekept.** Comfortable with vernacular names across
  languages; welcoming to newcomers to each tradition.
- **Honest about limits.** If a check wasn't done, we don't show a tick.

## Brand values

- **Trust** — verified sellers, honest labels, real reviews.
- **Authenticity** — correct names, real origins, real people selling.
- **Safety** — compliance is a feature, not friction.
- **Respect** — for cultures, customers and the law.
- **Access** — bringing scattered trade into one trusted home.

## Trust marks (use only when true)

- **✓ Verified Seller** — identity/business verified.
- **✓ Trusted Seller** — verified + track record.
- Never "MHRA approved" or similar unless the specific product genuinely holds
  THR/MA. Seller verification and product regulatory status are shown separately.

## Open items for design phase

- Logo (wordmark + leaf/root mark), app icon, splash.
- UI component library on the palette + type.
- Photography guidelines (warm, natural light, real product).
- Final commercial name confirmation with the sponsor.
