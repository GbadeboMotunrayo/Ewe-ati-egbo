# Ewe ati Egbo — Market & Regulatory Analysis

Source: PRD §2, §3, §5; `pitch.md`, `logistics.md`. Backs the "why now," the market
size for a pitch, and the regulatory + import classification that shapes the product.

## 1. The opportunity

The UK African/Caribbean diaspora (~2.5M+ people) buys traditional herbs, botanicals
and natural products continuously — food, wellbeing, culture. Demand is real and
visible (Etsy carries a live African-herbs category; IG/WhatsApp seller networks are
everywhere). But it's served by a broken patchwork:

- **Untrusted** — no verified sellers, inconsistent quality, no buyer protection.
- **Unstructured** — wrong/missing names, no ingredient/origin info, no compliance.
- **Logistically broken** — the hard part is moving product **Africa→UK** reliably,
  legally and affordably. That is the real pain and the real money.

## 2. The insight that makes this a venture, not an app

Everyone before us built a *storefront* — the easy 20%. The unsolved 80% is
**cross-border logistics + compliance for a fragmented, low-trust supply chain**.
Whoever owns that rail owns the category. So we build the rail (`logistics.md`) and the
storefront rides on top.

## 6. UK regulatory + import classification

Herbal products are **not one legal category**, and importing them adds a layer. The
platform classifies each product and applies both the domestic rule and the import rule
(enforced in `compliance.md` + `logistics.md`).

| Class | Regime & regulator | Import layer | Constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Herbs/botanicals (food)** | Food/general law; FSA | Food-import + possible plant-health rules | No medicinal claims |
| **B — Food supplements** | Food-supplement framework; FSA | Food-import rules | No disease claims |
| **C — Cosmetics** | UK Cosmetics Regulation; OPSS | Cosmetics safety on entry | Cosmetic-function claims only |
| **D — Herbal medicines** | Human Medicines Regs; MHRA (THR/MA) | Medicine import controls | Gated; closed by default at MVP |

Hard rules: (1) **claims are regulated** — medicinal claims blocked regardless of
contents; (2) **restricted/banned ingredients** blocked via a green/amber/red list;
(3) **import correctness** handled by the platform per class/corridor.

### 6.1 Platform-level obligations
- **Digital-platform reporting (HMRC/OECD model rules)** — collect/verify/report seller
  info; why verification is mandatory.
- **Consumer information** (pricing, delivery cost/ETA, returns) shown pre-purchase.
- **Fake reviews** (DMCC Act 2024) — verified-purchase-only + detection.
- **Online product safety + import** — restricted lists, recalls, correct declarations.

> Working understanding for scoping. A UK-qualified regulatory/customs adviser must
> confirm obligations per class and per corridor before launch. Not legal advice.

## 7. Competitors

| Competitor | Strength | What we exploit |
|---|---|---|
| Etsy | Reach, existing herb demand | No verification/structure/compliance/logistics |
| Amazon | Logistics (generic), checkout trust | No cultural specificity, no Africa→UK niche rail |
| Holland & Barrett | Trusted, compliant | No diaspora range, not a marketplace, no rail |
| IG/WhatsApp sellers | Authenticity, community | No trust, no structure, no reliable shipping |
| Local grocers / suitcase importers | Trust, authenticity | No reach, no scale, informal logistics |

## 8. Beachhead → expansion

West African + Caribbean UK diaspora (beachhead) → more African origins → US/EU
diaspora corridors. Reusable assets: the logistics rail, the compliance/import engine,
and the multilingual botanical dataset.
